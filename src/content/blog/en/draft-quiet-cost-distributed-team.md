Remote work did not fail us because of time zones. It failed in the gaps between meetings — the hallway questions, the whiteboard arguments, the shared silence when something broke at 2 a.m.

This draft collects three years of infrastructure work across continents: what we kept (async runbooks, written decisions), what we quietly gave up (spontaneous design reviews), and the small rituals that finally made the distance feel less brittle.
